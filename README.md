# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, seller performance, payment methods, and delivery operations.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Excel, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

- What is the overall sales and order performance?
- How many customers and sellers are represented in the dataset?
- Which states generate the most orders?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- Which product categories experience more late deliveries?
- Which sellers have the highest sales?
- What is the average delivery time?
- What are the major opportunities for improving business and operational performance?
